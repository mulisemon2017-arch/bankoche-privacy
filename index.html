Bankoche privacy policy

Last updated: 2026-10-27

What Bankoche does

Bankoche shows your income, spending and balances. It builds this picture from text messages (SMS) sent by the banks, wallets and phone numbers that you choose, or from messages you paste in by hand.

What it handles and where it stays

  • Text messages from the senders you add, and the amounts, balances, account digits and names it finds in them.
  • Messages you paste in by hand.
  • Names and settings you give to your sources, accounts you switch off, a monthly budget, debts you write down, and payments you type in.
  • An optional PIN. Only a salted one-way hash is kept, never the PIN itself.

All of this is stored only on your phone. Bankoche does not have the internet permission, so it cannot send anything anywhere. There is no account, no server, no analytics and no advertising. Phone backups and device-to-device transfer do not include Bankoche data.

Text message permission

Bankoche asks for the permissions RECEIVE_SMS and READ_SMS so it can read new and old messages from the senders you choose. It asks before it reads anything and explains why. You can say no and paste messages by hand instead. You can withdraw the permission at any time in the phone settings.

Messages it does not read

Messages from senders you did not add are dropped immediately: they are not parsed and not stored. To let you pick a sender when you add a source, the app reads only the sender column of your inbox, not message text.

Sharing

Bankoche shares nothing by itself. If you choose "Share as CSV", the file text goes to the app you pick.

Backup files

"Save backup" writes a file to a place you choose. It is plain text and not encrypted, so keep it private. "Restore" only reads a file you pick. Debts are not part of the backup file.

Your control

  • Turn a source off to hide it, or delete it to remove everything stored from it.
  • Switch off one account of a bank to leave it out of every total.
  • Dismiss items in "To check" to delete the stored message text.
  • Uninstalling the app removes all of its data.

Children

Bankoche is meant for adults and is not directed at children.
